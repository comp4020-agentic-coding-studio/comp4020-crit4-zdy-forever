Two Hands — a live browser instrument

Everything you hear is synthesised live in this page — nothing is pre-recorded.

  • Two registers: LOW (C3–C4) and HIGH (C4–C5), each its own wheel.
  • NOTE / CHORD: press a wheel's centre button to switch it between a single note and a triad per sector.
  • Play: use your keyboard, mouse, or touch on each sector — number keys 1 and 0 also toggle NOTE/CHORD.
  • Optional camera: click "Enable Camera" to play hands-free — an open palm shows a wheel, a fist locks it, and reaching in a direction plays that sector.
  • Piano plays many notes at once; Violin plays one at a time.
